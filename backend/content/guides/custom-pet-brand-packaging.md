Selling the same product as everyone else is a race to the bottom. Custom products with your logo — what the industry calls OEM — let you build a brand instead of a price comparison. China makes this remarkably accessible: logo printing and private-label packaging often start at just 100–200 units.

## The three levels of customization

### 1. Logo printing

Your logo applied to an existing product. Fastest turnaround (7–14 days), lowest MOQ, minimal cost — usually a few cents per unit.

### 2. Private-label packaging

A standard product in a box or sleeve printed with your brand. This is the highest-value upgrade per dollar: the same toy in branded packaging can retail for 30–50% more.

### 3. Full OEM

Your design, your mold, your packaging. Molds for injection-molded products run a few thousand dollars and 30–45 days. Only worth it once a product line has proven sales.

## What MOQ really means for custom orders

MOQ for customization is usually set by the printing process, not the product:

- **Sticker/hangtag logo:** often no MOQ increase at all
- **Silk-screen or pad printing:** 100–300 units
- **Full-color printed packaging:** 500–1,000 units per design run

> A practical starter move: order 200 units with a printed logo and sticker-sealed branded boxes. You get 80% of the brand effect at 20% of the commitment.

## Files and specs to prepare

Before requesting an OEM quote, have ready:

1. Vector logo file (AI, EPS, or SVG)
2. Preferred print positions and size in millimeters
3. Pantone or hex color codes
4. Packaging dimensions if you supply your own box design
